Beyond our shop: sustainable Vienna

We love it when you shop less. Here are real places in Vienna to thrift, swap and repair.

🏪

carla (Caritas)

Second-hand shops run by Caritas, e.g. Stephansplatz 5 (1010) and Mittersteig 10 (1050). Proceeds support social projects.

carla-wien.at →
♻️

48er-Tandler

The City of Vienna's own re-use shop: Siebenbrunnenfeldgasse 3, 1050. Clothes, books, furniture and more.

48ertandler.wien.gv.at →
👕

HUMANA

Vintage & second-hand chain with many Vienna stores, e.g. Lerchenfelder Straße 45 (1070).

humana.at →
🧵

Wiener Reparaturbon

The city covers 50% of repair costs (up to €100), e.g. for shoes. The 2026 budget is already used up, so watch for the next round.

wien.gv.at →
